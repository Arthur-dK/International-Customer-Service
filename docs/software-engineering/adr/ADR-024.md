# ADR 024: Confirm block and unblock, then a keypad after silence

## Context
Block and unblock played their result line as soon as the action word matched. The task menu had no keypad. Language selection already opens a keypad after silence, and speech can interrupt that keypad.

## Decision
- Block and unblock ask for yes or no before the result line. The words are English yes/no, French oui/non, Hebrew ken/כן and lo/לא, Arabic naam/نعم and la/لا, Swahili ndiyo/hapana. Only the selected language's words count, as whole tokens. Both answers in one utterance are not an answer.
- Speech that is not yes or no repeats the confirm prompt once, then the task menu plays. Silence does not count as that repeat.
- Six seconds of silence opens a keypad. Task keys are 1 balance, 2 PIN, 3 statement, 4 block, 5 unblock. Confirm keys are 1 yes and 2 no. Speech during that keypad is barge-in and is handled as speech. Choosing block or unblock from the keypad still asks for yes or no.
- Balance, PIN, and statement still play their line without a confirm step.

## Trade-off Analysis
- **Benefit:** A card is not blocked or unblocked on a single unclear sentence, and a caller who stays quiet can use the keypad.
- **Drawback:** The spoken retry is only one. A second unclear answer leaves the confirm and plays the task menu again.
