# ADR 007: SMS-owned English placeholders replace the echo

## Context
FEAT-01 copies an allowlisted text back to the sender. That echo was a placeholder. This feature answers with a fixed line for the matched card action. The IVR already has spoken lines for those actions, including a PIN line that says the PIN will not be spoken on the call. Those strings must not be the SMS replies. Nothing is stored between texts, so block and unblock cannot ask a later yes or no.

## Decision
- The reply strings live in an SMS module. They are not read from `core/language/phrases.json`.
- Every reply is English:
  - Balance: "I confirm you wish to check the balance. This is a placeholder."
  - PIN: "I confirm you wish to check the PIN. This is a placeholder."
  - Statement: "I confirm you wish to check the statement. This is a placeholder."
  - Block: "I confirm you wish to block the card. This is a placeholder."
  - Unblock: "I confirm you wish to unblock the card. This is a placeholder."
  - No match: "I do not know what you are talking about, please limit your requests to 1 message."
- The PIN line has no digits and no real PIN. None of the lines report a real balance or card status.
- An allowlisted non-empty body no longer gets the echo. One match, or the earlier of two matches, gets that action's line. No match gets the sentence above.
- A number that is not on the allowlist still gets the FEAT-01 not-recognised line. An empty body still sends nothing.
- One inbound text, one reply. The text is not stored for a follow-up. Block and unblock do not wait for confirmation.
- The send still uses the Messages API, with empty TwiML on the webhook, as in [ADR-001](ADR-001.md). `MessageSid` dedupe is unchanged.

## Trade-off Analysis
- **Benefit:** The phone gets a fixed English answer, and the spoken IVR catalog can change without changing SMS.
- **Drawback:** A sender who writes in another language still receives English. There is no second text to confirm a block or an unblock.
