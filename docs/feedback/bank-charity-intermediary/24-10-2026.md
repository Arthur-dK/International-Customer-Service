## Points

### 1 - Clearly-documented application requirements

I had listed a 'user experience' section under each `features/` file, but he found that to be a little misleading, since I was treating features as 'steps in the development cycle'. For a new person to properly test it, they would need a highly-detailed list of requirements that encapsulate any potential confusion towards the app's functionality. The aforementioned files also conflated the feature goals, and the technical decisions to implement those goals (put simply, the 'what' and the 'how').

I had created a basic list of requirements for each of the 3 functionalities, but they were not as detailed as anticipated, and not reviewed by any potential stakeholder.

**Disposition:** In progress. The lists are written. This source has not reviewed them yet.

**Resolution:** Tester-facing behavior is in [phone](../../requirements/ivr_requirements.md), [SMS](../../requirements/sms_requirements.md), and [email](../../requirements/email_requirements.md). `docs/software-engineering/` keeps the build logs and the ADRs.
